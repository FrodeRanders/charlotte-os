# Research context

- [Related systems and their afterlives](related-systems.md) compares the
  capability, microkernel, distributed-OS, multicore, recovery, networking,
  and cluster-management lineages cited by CharlotteOS. It distinguishes
  direct design sources, mechanisms adopted with changes, architectural
  influences, and ideas CharlotteOS explicitly narrows or rejects.
- [Cluster-native research lineages](cluster-native-lineages.md) reconnects
  the current placement, readiness, DSR, role-separated deployment, connector,
  lifecycle, and generated-application mechanisms to prior and current work,
  and states the experiments needed to test the resulting research claims.
